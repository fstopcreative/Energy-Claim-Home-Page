Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making the wbpdux website readable and usable for everyone.

Our approach

WBPDUX aims to make the site clear, readable and accessible across modern browsers and devices. Pages should use clear headings, sufficient contrast, descriptive links, keyboard-accessible forms, alt text for meaningful images and plain-language instructions.
